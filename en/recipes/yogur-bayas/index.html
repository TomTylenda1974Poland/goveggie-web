Yogurt with berries and toasted oats

Breakfast · Dessert · Vegetarian · For children

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Toast the oats

    Toast the oats in a dry skillet over medium heat for 4 minutes, stirring until nutty. Cool on a plate.

  2. 2. Prepare the berries

    Wash the fruit and cut up larger pieces. Crush half with the sugar to release a light syrup.

  3. 3. Build the layers

    Layer yogurt and fruit. For young children, flatten whole berries. Add the oats just before serving.

For little hands

Spoon the yogurt and fruit into layers.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
