Yogurt with berries and toasted oats

Breakfast · Dessert · Vegetarian · For children

Ingredients

Method

  1. Toast the oats in a dry skillet over medium heat for 4 minutes, stirring until nutty. Cool on a plate.
  2. Wash the fruit and cut up larger pieces. Crush half with the sugar to release a light syrup.
  3. Layer yogurt and fruit. For young children, flatten whole berries. Add the oats just before serving.
