Risotto with deeply browned mushrooms

Dinner · Vegan

Method

  1. Heat the stock. Brown the sliced mushrooms in half the oil for 6–8 minutes, leaving them undisturbed at first. Set aside.
  2. Soften the minced onion in the remaining oil. Stir in the rice for a minute. Add hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring as it is absorbed.
  3. Taste a grain after 16 minutes. When tender with a firm centre, fold in the mushrooms and parsley. Serve while the risotto still flows.
