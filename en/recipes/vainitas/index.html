Green beans with onion and lemon

Lunch · Small plates · Vegan

Method

  1. Trim the beans and cut long varieties into 5 cm lengths. Boil until tender with a little bite, 4–8 minutes depending on thickness. Drain.
  2. Brown thin onion slices in oil for 5 minutes. Toss with the beans and salt over the heat for 2 minutes.
  3. Add lemon juice off the heat and shake the pan to coat.
