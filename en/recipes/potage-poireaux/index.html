Leek and potato potage

Lunch · Dinner · Vegan · For children

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Wash and soften the leek

    Split the leek lengthwise and wash between its layers. Slice the white and pale green parts. Soften in oil for 7 minutes without browning.

  2. 2. Cook the potato

    Add cubed potato and stock. Simmer for 15–18 minutes until the potato crushes easily.

  3. 3. Blend the soup

    Blend briefly with an immersion blender to keep the soup light. Serve scattered with chopped parsley.

For little hands

After an adult splits the leek, rinse between the layers.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
