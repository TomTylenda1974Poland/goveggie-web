Roasted tomatillo salsa

Small plates · Vegan

Method

  1. Remove the tomatillo husks and rinse the fruit. Roast with the onion and garlic at 220 °C for 15–18 minutes until tender and lightly charred.
  2. Pulse with the salt and culantro. Add minced chili a speck at a time, tasting before adding more, especially with habanero.
  3. Rest for 5 minutes. Keep the texture chunky so it sits well on a tortilla.
