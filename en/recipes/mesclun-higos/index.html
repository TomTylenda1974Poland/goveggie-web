Warm figs over mixed leaves

Lunch · Small plates · Vegetarian

Ingredients

Method

  1. Halve the figs and sear cut-side down in a little of the oil for 2 minutes.
  2. Add honey and half the vinegar. Turn the figs in the glossy juices for 30 seconds and take off the heat.
  3. Whisk remaining vinegar and oil with salt. Dress the leaves and top with warm figs and the pan juices.
