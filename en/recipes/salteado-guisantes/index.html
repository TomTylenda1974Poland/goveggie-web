Snow peas and baby corn with ginger

Lunch · Dinner · Vegan

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Prepare the peas and corn

    String the snow peas and halve the baby corn lengthwise. Blanch the corn for 3 minutes and drain.

  2. 2. Stir-fry with ginger

    Heat the oil in a wide pan. Stir-fry the corn for 2 minutes, add peas and slivered ginger, and toss for another 3 minutes.

  3. 3. Add the tamari

    Pour tamari around the hot rim of the pan and toss. Finish with sesame oil off the heat.

For little hands

Pull the string from the snow peas with your fingers.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
