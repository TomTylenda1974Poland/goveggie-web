Shiitake and enoki broth with greens

Lunch · Dinner · Vegan

Method

  1. Remove tough shiitake stems and slice the caps. Trim the compact base from the enoki and separate the clusters. Wash the greens and separate stalks from leaves.
  2. Simmer the stock with sliced ginger and shiitake for 8 minutes. Add enoki and the stalks of the greens. Simmer for 6 minutes to cook the mushrooms thoroughly.
  3. Add the leaves and tamari. Cook for 2 more minutes and serve piping hot.
