Stone fruit clafoutis

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Prepare the dish and fruit

    Heat the oven to 180 °C and butter a small baking dish. Remove every pit. Halve cherries or thinly slice larger fruit.

  2. 2. Whisk the batter

    Whisk eggs with sugar, add flour, then pour in milk gradually to make a smooth batter. Arrange the fruit in the dish and pour over the batter.

  3. 3. Bake until set

    Bake for 25–32 minutes until the edges are golden and the centre is set. Cool for 5 minutes before serving.

For little hands

Butter the cool baking dish.
