Lychee and grapes in lemongrass syrup

Dessert · Vegan

Method

  1. Bruise the base of the lemongrass stalk. Boil water and sugar, turn off the heat, add the stalk and steep for 8 minutes. Strain and cool.
  2. Peel and pit each lychee or rambutan and halve the flesh. Halve the grapes and remove any seeds.
  3. Toss the fruit with the cold syrup and lemon juice. Chill for 10 minutes before serving.
