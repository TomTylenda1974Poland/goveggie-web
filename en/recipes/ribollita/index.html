Tuscan kale and white bean soup

Lunch · Dinner · Vegan

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Soften the vegetables

    Finely dice the celery, carrot and tender kale stems. Soften in the oil for 6 minutes. Stir in the chopped tomato.

  2. 2. Add the beans

    Mash a third of the beans. Add all the beans, stock and shredded kale leaves. Simmer gently for 15 minutes.

  3. 3. Thicken with bread

    Tear the bread into the pot. Cook for 3 minutes and rest for another 3 so the soup becomes thick and spoonable.

For little hands

Tear the bread into pieces over a plate.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
