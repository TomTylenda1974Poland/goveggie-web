Griddled vegetable skewers with paprika yogurt

Lunch · Dinner · Vegetarian

Ingredients

Tick what you already have. The rest goes on your shopping list.

Method

  1. Brush skewers with oil and salt. Cook on a hot griddle for 12–16 minutes, turning a quarter turn at a time until tender.
  2. Mix yogurt with paprika and lemon juice. Spoon the cool sauce beneath the vegetables straight off the griddle.
