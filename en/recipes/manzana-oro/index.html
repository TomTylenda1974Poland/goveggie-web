Ambarella and ginger relish

Small plates · Vegan

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Prepare the ambarella

    Peel the ambarella and cut flesh away from its fibrous stone. Discard the stone and finely chop the flesh.

  2. 2. Simmer with ginger

    Simmer with minced onion, grated ginger, vinegar, sugar, water and salt. Cover for 8 minutes, then uncover and cook for 6–8 more until thick and juicy.

  3. 3. Cool and serve

    Let it cool to lukewarm so the acidity mellows. Spoon over roasted vegetables.

For little hands

Measure the sugar and vinegar into separate cups.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
