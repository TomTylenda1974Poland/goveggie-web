Glazed turnips and radishes

Lunch · Small plates · Vegetarian

Method

  1. Peel the turnip and cut it into wedges the size of halved radishes. Arrange in a single layer in a skillet.
  2. Add butter, sugar, water and salt. Cover and simmer for 10 minutes or until tender to the tip of a knife.
  3. Uncover and swirl as the liquid evaporates. When the vegetables look glossy, add parsley and serve.
