Korean melon and star fruit with lemon

Small plates · Vegan

Ingredients

Method

  1. Wash the star fruit, trim any tough ridge edges and slice crosswise into thin stars. Remove the seeds.
  2. Deseed the melon and slice the flesh into crescents. Arrange both fruits on a cool plate.
  3. Gently bruise the mint with the lemon juice and a few teaspoons of juice from the melon. Spoon over the fruit and serve.
