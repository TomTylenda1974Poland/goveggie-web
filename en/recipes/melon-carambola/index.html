Korean melon and star fruit with lemon

Small plates · Vegan

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Slice the star fruit

    Wash the star fruit, trim any tough ridge edges and slice crosswise into thin stars. Remove the seeds.

  2. 2. Prepare the melon

    Deseed the melon and slice the flesh into crescents. Arrange both fruits on a cool plate.

  3. 3. Add lemon and mint

    Gently bruise the mint with the lemon juice and a few teaspoons of juice from the melon. Spoon over the fruit and serve.

For little hands

Arrange the stars and melon crescents on the plate.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
