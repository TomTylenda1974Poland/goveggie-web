Red beans with ají gustoso

Lunch · Dinner · Vegan

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Soften the aromatics

    Soften chopped onion in the oil for 5 minutes. Add deseeded pepper, grated tomato and the thyme sprig. Cook until thickened.

  2. 2. Thicken the beans

    Add beans, water and salt. Mash two spoonfuls of beans against the pot and simmer for 15 minutes.

  3. 3. Add the culantro

    Remove the sprig. Stir in finely chopped culantro off the heat to keep its aroma fresh.

For little hands

Wash the culantro leaves.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
