Coconut rice pudding with mango

Breakfast · Dessert · Vegan · For children

Method

  1. Simmer the rice in the water, covered, for 16–18 minutes until very soft.
  2. Add coconut milk, sugar and cinnamon. Stir over low heat for 10–12 minutes until a spoon drawn through leaves a brief trail.
  3. Cool to lukewarm. Dice half the mango and mash the rest. Spoon the mash beneath the rice and scatter the cubes on top.
