Orange-braised fennel

Lunch · Dinner · Vegan

Method

  1. Cut the bulb into wedges, keeping the base attached. Slice the stalks into very thin rounds. Brown the flat faces of the wedges in the oil.
  2. Add the stalks, stock, salt, orange juice and a thin strip of zest. Cover and braise for 18–22 minutes until tender.
  3. Remove the zest. Uncover and reduce the liquid until it coats a spoon. Spoon it over the wedges just before serving.
