Avocado toast with fresh tomato

Breakfast · Small plates · Vegan

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Toast the bread

    Toast the bread on both sides. Grate half the tomato and thinly slice the rest.

  2. 2. Mash the avocado

    Mash the avocado with lemon juice and half the salt, leaving some texture. Rub the grated tomato over the toast, then top with avocado.

  3. 3. Add the tomato

    Fan the tomato slices over the toast and finish with the remaining salt just before serving.

For little hands

Mash the avocado in a bowl.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
