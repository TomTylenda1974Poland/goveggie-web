Dominican · 40 min

Squash rice with culantro

Lunch · Dinner · Vegan · For children

Ingredients

Method

  1. Cut the squash into 1.5 cm cubes. Brown in the oil for 5 minutes and reserve half to finish.
  2. Soften the diced onion with the remaining squash for 4 minutes. Add minced garlic, rinsed rice, water and salt. Boil until the rice shows above the liquid, about 5 minutes.
  3. Cover, turn the heat to low and cook for 15 minutes. Rest covered for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork and fold in the browned squash and finely sliced culantro.
