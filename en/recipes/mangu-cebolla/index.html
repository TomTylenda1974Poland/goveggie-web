Dominican · 35 min

Mangú with tangy onions

Breakfast · Vegan · For children

Method

  1. Boil the plantain chunks in water with half the salt until a fork slides through, 20–25 minutes.
  2. Thinly slice the onion. Soften in half the oil for 4 minutes, splash in the vinegar and take off the heat.
  3. Reserve some cooking water. Mash the hot plantain with the remaining oil and salt. Add cooking water a spoonful at a time until silky. Spoon the onions and their juices over the top.
