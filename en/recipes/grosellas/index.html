Gooseberry and orange compote

Dessert · Vegan

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Prepare the berries and orange

    Top and tail the fruit and wash well. Peel a strip of orange zest without the white pith and squeeze the orange.

  2. 2. Simmer until syrupy

    Simmer the gooseberries with the juice, zest, water and sugar for 12–15 minutes until they split and the syrup coats a spoon.

  3. 3. Press through a sieve

    Remove the zest and push through a coarse sieve to remove hard seeds, especially with ketembilla. Serve chilled.

For little hands

Rinse the gooseberries with water.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
