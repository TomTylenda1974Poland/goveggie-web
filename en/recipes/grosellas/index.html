Gooseberry and orange compote

Dessert · Vegan

Method

  1. Top and tail the fruit and wash well. Peel a strip of orange zest without the white pith and squeeze the orange.
  2. Simmer the gooseberries with the juice, zest, water and sugar for 12–15 minutes until they split and the syrup coats a spoon.
  3. Remove the zest and push through a coarse sieve to remove hard seeds, especially with ketembilla. Serve chilled.
