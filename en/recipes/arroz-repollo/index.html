Fried rice with napa cabbage and oyster mushrooms

Dinner · Vegan

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Prepare the rice and vegetables

    Spread the freshly cooked rice on a wide plate while you prep. Thinly shred the cabbage and tear the mushrooms into slender strips.

  2. 2. Brown the mushrooms and cabbage

    Brown the mushrooms in half the oil for 6 minutes. Add the remaining oil and cabbage. Cook over high heat for 3 minutes.

  3. 3. Stir-fry the rice

    Add rice, tamari and sliced scallions. Stir-fry until piping hot and the grains separate.

For little hands

Tear the oyster mushrooms apart with clean hands.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
