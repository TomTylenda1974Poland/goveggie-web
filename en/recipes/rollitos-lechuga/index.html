Lettuce cups with lemon chickpeas

Lunch · Small plates · Vegan

Ingredients

Tick what you already have. The rest goes on your shopping list.

Method

  1. Wash and dry whole leaves. If small or curly, overlap a pair to make each cup.
  2. Roughly mash chickpeas with oil, lemon juice and salt. Dice the cucumber and chop the dill, then fold into the chickpeas.
  3. Fill the leaves just before serving so they stay crisp and cool.
