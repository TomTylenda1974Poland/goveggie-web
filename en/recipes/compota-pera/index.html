Apple and pear compote with vanilla

Breakfast · Dessert · Vegan · For children

Method

  1. Peel the fruit for a smoother texture. Core and cut into small, even dice.
  2. Cook with the water and vanilla, covered over low heat, for 12–16 minutes. Stir once halfway through.
  3. Uncover to evaporate excess liquid. Mash only half, add lemon juice and fold it back through the remaining fruit.
