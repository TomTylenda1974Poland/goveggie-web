Garlicky portobellos on white bean purée

Dinner · Vegan

Ingredients

Method

  1. Slice the elephant garlic paper-thin. Warm in the oil over low heat until just golden. Lift out the garlic and save the oil.
  2. Thickly slice whole portobellos. Sear in the garlic oil for 4 minutes on each side.
  3. Heat the beans with the stock and blend with the lemon juice. Serve the mushrooms over the purée with the crisp garlic and parsley.
