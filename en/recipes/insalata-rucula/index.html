Arugula with pear and toasted macadamias

Lunch · Small plates · Vegan

Ingredients

Method

  1. Toast the macadamias in a dry skillet over medium-low heat for 4 minutes. Cool and chop coarsely.
  2. Thinly slice the pear and toss with half the lemon juice. Whisk the remaining juice with the oil and salt.
  3. Dress the arugula just before serving. Layer with the pear slices and scatter the nuts on top.
