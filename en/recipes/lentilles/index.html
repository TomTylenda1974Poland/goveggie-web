Warm lentils with beetroot and watercress

Lunch · Dinner · Vegan

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Cook the beetroot

    Peel the beetroot, cut into 1 cm cubes and simmer in water for 15–20 minutes until tender. Drain.

  2. 2. Dress the lentils

    Warm the lentils with a spoonful of water. Mix vinegar, oil, minced onion and salt. Dress the lentils while warm.

  3. 3. Finish with watercress

    Fold in the beetroot and add washed, dried watercress on top just before serving for contrasting textures.

For little hands

Wash and dry the watercress.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
