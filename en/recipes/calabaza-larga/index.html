Long gourd and lentil stew

Lunch · Dinner · Vegan

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Prepare the gourd

    Use a young, mild gourd. If a normally mild gourd tastes unusually bitter, discard it. Peel and dice the flesh.

  2. 2. Soften the aromatics

    Warm cumin and minced ginger in the oil for 30 seconds. Add chopped tomato and cook until it breaks down.

  3. 3. Simmer with lentils

    Add gourd, lentils and stock. Cover and simmer for 15–20 minutes until the cubes are easily pierced.

For little hands

Measure the lentils into a bowl.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
