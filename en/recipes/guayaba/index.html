Guava in lemon balm syrup

Dessert · Vegan

Method

  1. Halve the guavas. Scoop out the seedy centres and rub through a sieve with a spoon. Reserve the purée and cut the shells into wedges.
  2. Boil the water and sugar for 3 minutes. Add wedges and purée and simmer for 12–15 minutes until tender.
  3. Turn off the heat and add lemon balm and lemon juice. Remove the leaves after 4 minutes. Serve warm or chilled.
