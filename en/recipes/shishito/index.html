Blistered shishitos with lemon

Small plates · Vegan

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Dry and oil the peppers

    Dry the peppers thoroughly and coat with oil. Heat a cast-iron skillet until very hot.

  2. 2. Blister and finish with lemon

    Cook in one layer for 4–6 minutes, turning with tongs as blisters form. Season with salt and lemon. Hold by the stem and discard it after eating. Some peppers can be hot.

For little hands

Pat the washed peppers dry with a clean towel.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
