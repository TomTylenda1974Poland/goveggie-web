Granadilla and kiwi cups with mint

Breakfast · Dessert · Vegan

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Prepare the granadilla and kiwi

    Scoop out ripe granadilla pulp, leaving the husk and white pith behind. Peel and dice the kiwi.

  2. 2. Add mint and serve

    Shred the mint just before using it. Toss with lemon juice and kiwi. Divide between glasses and spoon the granadilla pulp on top.

For little hands

Scoop out the pulp after an adult opens the granadilla.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
