Creamy oats with warm raspberries

Breakfast · Vegan · For children

Method

  1. Put raspberries, sugar and a spoonful of the water in a saucepan. If frozen, start on low until they release juice. Bring to a boil and boil for 2 minutes.
  2. In another pan, cook the oats with the remaining water and coconut milk, stirring for 6–8 minutes until creamy.
  3. Spoon the oats into bowls and swirl the warm fruit through each serving.
