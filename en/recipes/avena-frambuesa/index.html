Creamy oats with warm raspberries

Breakfast · Vegan · For children

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Simmer the raspberries

    Put raspberries, sugar and a spoonful of the water in a saucepan. If frozen, start on low until they release juice. Bring to a boil and boil for 2 minutes.

  2. 2. Cook the oats

    In another pan, cook the oats with the remaining water and coconut milk, stirring for 6–8 minutes until creamy.

  3. 3. Swirl in the fruit

    Spoon the oats into bowls and swirl the warm fruit through each serving.

For little hands

Measure the oats while an adult prepares the fruit.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
