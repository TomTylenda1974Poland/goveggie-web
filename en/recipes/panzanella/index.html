Tomato and cucumber panzanella

Lunch · Vegan

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Toast the bread and salt the tomatoes

    Cube the bread and toast at 190 °C for 8 minutes. Cut the tomatoes over a bowl to catch the juice and season with salt.

  2. 2. Make the dressing

    Whisk vinegar and oil into the juice. Toss the bread with the dressing and leave for 5 minutes so the outside softens while the centre keeps some crunch.

  3. 3. Toss and rest

    Fold in the tomatoes, cucumber chunks, thinly sliced onion and torn basil. Serve at room temperature.

For little hands

Tear the basil leaves by hand.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
