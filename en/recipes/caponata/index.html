Sweet and sour eggplant with celery

Lunch · Small plates · Vegan

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Brown the eggplant cubes

    Brown the cubed eggplant in the oil in a skillet, working in batches if needed. Remove when tender.

  2. 2. Cook the celery with tomato

    Soften thinly sliced celery in the same pan for 4 minutes. Add halved tomatoes and salt and cook until they give up their juices.

  3. 3. Add the sweet and sour dressing

    Dissolve the sugar in the vinegar and add with the eggplant. Cook for 5 more minutes and let it cool to lukewarm before serving.

For little hands

Stir the sugar into the vinegar in a bowl.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
