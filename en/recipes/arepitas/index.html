Pan-fried corn cakes with scallions

Breakfast · Small plates · Vegetarian · For children

Method

  1. Bring water and salt to a boil. Sprinkle in the cornmeal, stirring constantly. Cook for 2–3 minutes until the thick dough pulls away from the pan.
  2. Transfer to a bowl and cool until lukewarm. Stir in the beaten egg and finely sliced scallions. Shape thin cakes using a damp spoon.
  3. Heat the oil over medium heat. Pan-fry the cakes for 3–4 minutes per side until firm and cooked through the centre. Serve warm.
