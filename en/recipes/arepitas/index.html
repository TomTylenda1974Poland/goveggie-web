Pan-fried corn cakes with scallions

Breakfast · Small plates · Vegetarian · For children

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Cook the cornmeal

    Bring water and salt to a boil. Sprinkle in the cornmeal, stirring constantly. Cook for 2–3 minutes until the thick dough pulls away from the pan.

  2. 2. Shape the cakes

    Transfer to a bowl and cool until lukewarm. Stir in the beaten egg and finely sliced scallions. Shape thin cakes using a damp spoon.

  3. 3. Fry on both sides

    Heat the oil over medium heat. Pan-fry the cakes for 3–4 minutes per side until firm and cooked through the centre. Serve warm.

For little hands

Once an adult checks the dough is lukewarm, pat it into discs.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
