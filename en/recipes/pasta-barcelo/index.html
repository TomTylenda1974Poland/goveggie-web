Pasta al pomodoro with basil

Lunch · Dinner · Vegan · For children

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Make the tomato sauce

    Crush the garlic and warm gently in the oil. When fragrant, add grated tomatoes and salt. Cook uncovered for 18–20 minutes.

  2. 2. Cook the pasta

    Cook the pasta according to the packet, stopping one minute early. Reserve a cup of cooking water and drain.

  3. 3. Toss and add basil

    Finish the pasta in the sauce, tossing and adding cooking water by the spoonful until glossy. Tear the basil over the top.

For little hands

Pick the basil leaves by hand.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
