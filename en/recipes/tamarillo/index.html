Cinnamon-poached tamarillo

Dessert · Vegan

Method

  1. Score a shallow cross in each fruit. Blanch in boiling water for one minute, cool and peel.
  2. Boil the measured water with sugar and cinnamon for 3 minutes. Halve the tamarillos and gently poach in the syrup for 6–8 minutes.
  3. Lift out the fruit and reduce the liquid until slightly syrupy. Spoon over the halves and serve at room temperature.
