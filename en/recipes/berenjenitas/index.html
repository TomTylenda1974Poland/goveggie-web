Small eggplants in coconut and ginger broth

Dinner · Vegan

Method

  1. Quarter the eggplants. Bruise the lemongrass with the back of a knife and leave it in one large piece for easy removal.
  2. Brown the cut faces in oil. Add sliced ginger, lemongrass, stock and coconut milk. Cover and simmer for 14–18 minutes until the flesh is creamy.
  3. Discard the lemongrass. Add lemon juice off the heat and serve in the hot broth.
