Small eggplants in coconut and ginger broth

Dinner · Vegan

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Prepare the eggplants

    Quarter the eggplants. Bruise the lemongrass with the back of a knife and leave it in one large piece for easy removal.

  2. 2. Brown and add the broth

    Brown the cut faces in oil. Add sliced ginger, lemongrass, stock and coconut milk. Cover and simmer for 14–18 minutes until the flesh is creamy.

  3. 3. Remove the lemongrass and serve

    Discard the lemongrass. Add lemon juice off the heat and serve in the hot broth.

For little hands

Measure the coconut milk into a small jug.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
