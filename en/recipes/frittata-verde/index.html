Spinach and green garlic frittata

Breakfast · Dinner · Vegetarian · For children

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Soften the green garlic

    Finely slice the green garlic. Soften in the oil in a lidded skillet sized for the eggs for 3 minutes.

  2. 2. Add spinach and eggs

    Add washed, chopped spinach and cook until the liquid evaporates. Beat eggs with salt and pour over the vegetables.

  3. 3. Cook until set

    Cover over low heat for 8–12 minutes until the centre is fully set. Rest for 2 minutes and slice into wedges.

For little hands

Rinse the spinach in a colander.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
