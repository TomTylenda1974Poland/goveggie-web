Chilled coconut cream with passion fruit

Dessert · Vegan

Ingredients

Method

  1. Dissolve the cornstarch in a little cold coconut milk. Add remaining milk and half the sugar. Stir until it boils and thickens, then cook for one more minute.
  2. Pour into small glasses and refrigerate for about 80 minutes until cold and set.
  3. Heat the passion fruit pulp with remaining sugar for 3 minutes and cool. Spoon a thin layer over each cream to serve.
