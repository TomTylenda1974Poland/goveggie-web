Roast cauliflower with parsley cream

Lunch · Dinner · Vegetarian

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Prepare the florets

    Heat the oven to 220 °C. Separate cauliflower into florets and slice the stalk into thin coins. Toss with oil and salt.

  2. 2. Roast and make the cream

    Roast for 22–26 minutes, turning once to brown multiple sides. Mix yogurt with finely chopped parsley and lemon juice.

  3. 3. Assemble the plate

    Spread the cool cream on a plate and scatter the hot cauliflower over it just before serving.

For little hands

Mix the yogurt and parsley in a cool bowl.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
