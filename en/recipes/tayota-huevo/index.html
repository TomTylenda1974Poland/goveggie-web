Chayote and soft-set eggs

Breakfast · Lunch · Vegetarian · For children

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Cook the chayote cubes

    Peel and core the chayote, then dice small. Boil for 7–9 minutes until tender and drain thoroughly.

  2. 2. Sauté with onion

    Soften the diced onion in the oil for 4 minutes. Add the chayote and sauté for 3 minutes to cook off moisture.

  3. 3. Fold in the eggs

    Beat the eggs with the salt. Fold through over low heat with a spatula until completely set.

For little hands

Measure the water for cooking the chayote.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
