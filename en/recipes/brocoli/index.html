Broccoli with crisp cornmeal and lemon

Lunch · Dinner · Vegan · For children

Ingredients

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 215 °C. Break the broccoli into small florets and peel and slice the stalk into thin batons.
  2. Toss with oil and salt. Dust lightly with cornmeal and spread on a roomy baking tray.
  3. Roast for 18–22 minutes, turning halfway for crisp edges. Add lemon juice at the table.
