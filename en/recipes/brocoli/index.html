Broccoli with crisp cornmeal and lemon

Lunch · Dinner · Vegan · For children

Step-by-step method

  1. 1. Separate the florets

    Heat the oven to 215 °C. Break the broccoli into small florets and peel and slice the stalk into thin batons.

  2. 2. Coat with cornmeal

    Toss with oil and salt. Dust lightly with cornmeal and spread on a roomy baking tray.

  3. 3. Roast until crisp

    Roast for 18–22 minutes, turning halfway for crisp edges. Add lemon juice at the table.

For little hands

Toss the broccoli with the oil in a cool bowl.

Let an adult handle knives, heat and electrical appliances.
