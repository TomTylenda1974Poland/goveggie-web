# First 30 days: social content plan

## Scope and publication gates

Planning window: December 2026, days 1–30; shift the schedule if approvals are late. This is a prototype-awareness plan, not a launch announcement. The five named products are prototypes, not on sale and not an approved launch range. Mock inventory is never evidence of availability. Existing illustrative website images are not real product footage.

Use the approved endorsement “Una marca de Go Veggie Life” / “A brand of Go Veggie Life”. Go Veggie Life is the main ingredient supplier. Product-specific ingredient origins, distribution agreements and manufacturing need separate supporting records.

Ingredients, allergens, net content, heat level, shelf life and storage are unconfirmed. Product names identify prototypes; they do not establish a complete ingredient list. Do not add dietary, nutrition, purity or similar claims. Do not announce sales, take orders, publish a contact number or invite purchase by DM. No public contact channel is confirmed.

Every named recipe is **draft — kitchen testing required**. Application captions remain editorial drafts until a responsible cook tests the actual combination and approves the page. Until then, retain “Idea en desarrollo” / “Idea in development” on screen and in captions; never call it tested or a customer favourite. Testing a dish does not approve a product for sale.

{SITE} is the undecided final production domain. Every URL below is a planning template, not a verified live page. Before publishing: confirm domain, route, page accuracy, link placement and analytics consent/configuration. Hold posts with broken or unapproved destinations. No development or checkout links.

## Goals, funnel and measurement

Help viewers understand a use, save an idea and explore accurate product information. Funnel: **social video → recipe page → product page → where to buy**. The last step becomes actionable only after a stockist is confirmed; until then its page must clearly state that availability is unconfirmed.

Record weekly by brief and platform: publication date, reach/views as reported by the platform, saves, shares and outbound link clicks. Record recipe landing-page visits with UTMs when analytics is available; keep visits separate from platform clicks. Compare saves/reach and shares/reach within the same platform and format, with denominators shown. Track recipe-to-product and product-to-where-to-buy clicks only if those events are actually configured; otherwise record “not instrumented.” No sales attribution, invented benchmarks or inferred purchases.

Use one simple tracking sheet; note missing data rather than estimating it. Review link placement as well as creative when clicks are low. Different platform view definitions are not directly comparable.

## Mix to test

| Pillar | Hypothesis allocation | What to learn |
|---|---:|---|
| Recipes and serving ideas | 50% | Which meal contexts earn saves, shares and recipe visits? |
| Real ingredients, preparation and people | 25% | Which real actions or introductions retain attention and lead to site clicks? |
| Genuine customers, chefs, tastings and retail partners | 15% | Do permissioned contributions prompt useful engagement and visits? |
| Availability and announcements | 10% | Once verified, do store posts lead to where-to-buy visits? |

These are hypotheses, not quotas. Fourteen posts cannot reproduce those percentages exactly: this prototype month deliberately overweights applications and includes only one optional partner/tasting slot. Do not create testimonials or manufacture a retail relationship to fill the mix. Replace unavailable partner/store slots with approved application footage or skip them. At day 30 compare each pillar's totals and per-post results, normalize by reach where possible, and note sample sizes, posting frequency and approval delays. Move one future slot toward the best-supported use case, then test again; do not infer causation from a single successful post.

## Link and production conventions

All calendar URLs are Instagram masters. For the Facebook version replace only `utm_source=instagram` with `utm_source=facebook`; for a TikTok reuse use `tiktok`. Use `whatsapp` only for an approved WhatsApp distribution channel, which does not currently exist. Keep `utm_medium=social`, campaign `2026-12_{theme}` and content equal to the brief ID. Themes below are `ideas`, `process`, `people`, `community`, `availability`.

Instagram: place the exact tracked destination in an available profile link or accompanying Story link and identify that placement in the caption before release. Facebook: use its own tracked link in the accompanying post. TikTok reuse is optional and depends on an available link surface; never imply a caption URL is clickable. Keep one CTA per post. English adaptations use the same verified destination; do not invent English recipe routes. Publish Spanish first, with English as a separate adaptation or clearly separated caption.

Aim for 15–25-second application edits, 20–30-second process/team edits and a 15-second store edit. Capture clean 9:16 footage without platform watermarks, readable subtitles and a clean master. Use approved music or original sound.
