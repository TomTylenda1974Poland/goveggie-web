# 02: Brand relationship recommendation

## Recommendation

Palapa Verde stays the **dominant consumer brand**. Go Veggie Life appears as a smaller endorsement or as an explanation, never as a co-equal logo.

The owner confirmed the brand relationship on 6 November 2026. The website uses **“Una marca de Go Veggie Life”** in Spanish and **“A brand of Go Veggie Life”** in English. Palapa Verde is the consumer-facing name. Go Veggie Life is its main ingredient supplier. Claims about a specific vegetable, product or batch require a matching source record. Distribution arrangements, the actual manufacturer and the registration holder require separate confirmation.

The website uses the owned setting in src/content/brand.ts. MANUFACTURER and REGISTRATION_HOLDER remain separate, empty fields pending confirmation.

The owner chose variant B, “Lekka palapa”, on 6 November 2026. The centered two-stroke roof spans 28% of the visible PALAPA VERDE wordmark. In the website logo, place the Go Veggie Life endorsement directly below the name in smaller italic lettering, with the same letter size in Spanish and English. The Spanish endorsement spans 72% of the wordmark and the English one approximately 65%, with a softer ink tone than the name. Use the approved SVG in each language.

## Visual reference for labels and photography

Use the reference board for typography, botanical drawings, photography and color. Every sticker has four straight edges and square corners, with no arched top or custom contour. Print a fine inset rectangular rule on white or warm off-white stock. Place the small black botanical engraving to the left of the product name in the middle of the sticker. For the first paper fitting use 100 × 75 mm (width × height), with a 100 × 100 mm square for comparison. Confirm the final size separately on the jar and the tub. Give the engraving and the two-stroke roof separate space. Set the brand and product name in strong serif lettering, with the product type and flavor easy to read at shelf distance.

On labels use the Palapa Verde B roof and wordmark on their own at the top. Place the Go Veggie Life packaging signature at the very bottom, after the product information, with clear space around it. Use the complete original symbol and “goveggie.life” lettering as one graphic. The accompanying words are “Una marca de” in Spanish and “by” in English. The photo series uses the website signature from web.goveggie.life: its original color symbol (goveggie-mark-v1.png) next to the lowercase wordmark in Archivo Black. Keep the same arrangement across products and change the engraving with the ingredient. Color comes from the food visible through its clear container and from real ingredients in the photographs. On the website, carry that contrast into the product presentation: pale space, dark type, colored food and restrained green accents.

Product names, weights, storage directions and other package copy come from the approved record for each product. The reference board supplies the visual style.

## Where the two sites link
- Palapa Verde: the "Nosotros" relationship section, the home relationship block, the footer, and the business page (for fresh produce).
- Go Veggie Life (separate task, root site): its footer already links to Palapa Verde. Recommended: a short "Palapa Verde" block on "Nosotros" or "Para supermercados" that uses the same confirmed wording.

## Packaging label hierarchy (recommendation for the packaging task, not applied)
1. Two-stroke roof centered above the **PALAPA VERDE** wordmark
2. **PALAPA VERDE** as the largest brand element
3. Thin rule
4. Botanical engraving to the left of the product name, e.g. “HUMMUS / de zanahoria”
5. Short descriptor, when approved
6. Confirmed net content and storage instruction in the product information area
7. Last row on the front: small introductory text followed by the complete Go Veggie Life packaging logo. Check it on the curved pack at actual size.
8. Back label: ingredients, allergens ("Contiene: …"), manufacturer name and address, lot/date, sanitary registration once issued, under NORDOM 53 review
